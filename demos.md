# Live demos

*Everything here runs in the browser — one click, no install. Supporting evidence for
the "range" line on the [landing page](README.md): GPU physics, generative geometry, and
a few interactive-math toys. The serious work is in the
[Lean](lean.md) / [verification](verification.md) / [expository](expository.md) pages.*

The gallery hub: **[raggedr.github.io](https://raggedr.github.io/)**.

## Physics & fluids (GPU / WebGL)
| Demo | What you see |
|------|--------------|
| [smoke-flow](https://raggedr.github.io/smoke-flow/) | Real-time GPU smoke: the laminar-to-turbulent transition, via Navier–Stokes. |
| [fire](https://raggedr.github.io/fire/) | Real-time fire: buoyant Navier–Stokes with vorticity confinement, combustion, and blackbody emission — one self-contained WebGL2 file. |
| [reaction-diffusion-screensaver](https://raggedr.github.io/reaction-diffusion-screensaver/) | Music-reactive Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion: maze patterns with beat-triggered ripples. |

## Generative & geometric art
| Demo | What you see |
|------|--------------|
| [kaleidescope](https://raggedr.github.io/kaleidescope/) | A six-fold kaleidoscope — the dihedral group D₆ — in a single dependency-free HTML file. |
| [hexstar](https://raggedr.github.io/hexstar/) | Stars of David emerging from a hexagonal tessellation — a geometric screensaver. |
| [dmt](https://raggedr.github.io/dmt/) | Animated recursive geometry: Zentangle-Paradox spirals and tunnel vortices, from pure straight lines. |
| [dancing](https://raggedr.github.io/dancing/) | Dance motion extracted from video, fitted to a cosine series, and analysed. |
| [screen-saver](https://raggedr.github.io/screen-saver/) | A collection of browser screensavers, tilings, and geometric art. |

## Interactive math & physics
| Demo | What you see |
|------|--------------|
| [belt-trick](https://raggedr.github.io/belt-trick/) | The Dirac belt trick in 3D — why spinors need 720°, as an SU(2) contraction (Three.js). |
| [stomachion](https://raggedr.github.io/stomachion/) | Archimedes' Stomachion puzzle — grab and move the wooden pieces; an exact-cover solver underneath. |
